## Two clocks

The moon in this website follows real time. The sea follows an artistic clock. Keeping the distinction visible makes the scene more honest, and perhaps more interesting.

Astronomy gives us the Sun–Moon elongation and the illuminated fraction. A small wave model gives us moving textures, overlapping frequencies, and a reflection that never quite holds still.

## A tide is local

Real tides depend on coastlines, water depth, resonances, and local harmonic constituents. A decorative ocean cannot replace a tide table.

The familiar spring–neap cycle offers a useful visual rhythm:

$$
A(\psi)=A_0+A_1\cos(2\psi).
$$

Here $\psi$ is the lunar elongation. This is a conceptual modulation, not a calibrated prediction.
